# About Me

My name is Tran Tuan Khang Nguyen. I work across data science, data analysis, business analysis, and business administration, with a professional focus on turning messy business problems into clear analytical systems.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Data Science at Seattle Pacific University. My technical foundation includes Python, R, machine learning, statistical modeling, data visualization, and working with large-scale datasets. Before that, I completed an Associate of Science Track II in Computer Science at Highline College.
